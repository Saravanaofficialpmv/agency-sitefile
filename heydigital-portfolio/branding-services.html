Memorable Brand Identity Creation

A brand is more than a logo design. It represents the values, promises, visual voice, and identity of your company across every digital and offline touchpoint. At Hey Digital, we design creative brand guidelines that help corporate entities build trust and stand out in competitive markets. We select color systems, structure typography parameters, and build templates for visual unity.

Our branding services cover logo styling, packaging layout design, labels mapping, dielines configuration, print templates, and corporate guidelines books. We ensure your visual identity aligns with your business goals and represents your brand voice cleanly.

Why Consistent Visual Systems Scale Sales

A visually inconsistent brand causes confusion. If your social profiles use different typography than your product boxes, or if your site uses mismatched colors, it drops customer trust. A unified visual style system helps build brand recognition. When users recognize your color schemes and icon styles instantly, it reinforces brand recall and trust.

Our design team structures layouts with visual balance. We map fonts to establish clean reading flows, set color contrast rules for readability, and design brand books. This keeps your messaging aligned, optimizes print templates, and builds corporate value.

Our Branding Workflow

We deliver creative brand designs using a structured roadmap:

  • 1. Brand Identity Analysis: Researching target audiences and competitor branding layouts.
  • 2. Typography & Color Audits: Selecting font styles and high-contrast color palettes.
  • 3. Logo & Visual Design: Drafting logo options, testing icons files, and building layouts.
  • 4. Brand Book Compilation: Outlining spacing rules, style constraints, and delivering print-ready files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Review common questions regarding our custom branding and corporate identity design services.

What is included in a corporate branding package?

+
-

Our corporate branding package includes primary and secondary logo design files, customized color palettes, typography selections, brand asset guidelines, print templates (business cards, letterheads), and social profile mockups.

How long does a brand design project take?

+
-

Branding design projects usually take 3 to 6 weeks. This includes audience research, typography audits, design iterations, and compiling your final brand guidelines book.

+
-

Yes. Once payment is finalized, full intellectual copyright ownership of the design works is transferred to your organization. We deliver all raw vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) for future scaling.

Do you design product packaging and retail labels?

+
-

Yes. We design custom product labels, custom structural package outlines (dielines), and shelf-ready box graphics to build visual presence in offline retail stores.
