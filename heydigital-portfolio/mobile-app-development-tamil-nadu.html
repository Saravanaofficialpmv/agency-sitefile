Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our mobile app development services? Find answers to commonly asked questions here, or contact our support team.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

+
-

Building a mobile application typically takes between 6 to 20+ weeks. A simple app with basic screens can be completed in about 6-8 weeks, while cross-platform Flutter systems or native iOS/Android builds with complex database schemas, third-party APIs, and real-time syncing can take 12-24 weeks.

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

+
-

Yes, we specialize in cross-platform mobile development using Flutter, allowing us to deploy native-performance apps on both iOS and Android from a single optimized codebase, reducing development time and cost.

How do you handle mobile app support and updates?

+
-

We provide post-launch maintenance packages to monitor store analytics, resolve operating system compatibility updates, optimize security protocols, and deploy feature updates as your business scales.
